Nugget: AI Flashcards & Tutor

Legal documents and support

Nugget is an iOS study app. You record a lecture or feed it a PDF, photos or pasted notes; it turns them into clean notes and flashcards in one deck, then schedules your reviews and tests you until it sticks. There is no account: your decks, notes, cards and recordings live on your iPhone.

Nugget is in development and not yet on the App Store. These documents describe the app as it is being built, and will be updated before it is released.
