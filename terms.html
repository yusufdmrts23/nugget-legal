4. AI-generated notes and cards

This section matters more than the rest, so please read it.

  • Notes and cards can be wrong. They are written by an AI model, either Apple's on-device model or Anthropic's Claude. They may leave out something important, misread a recording or a page, or state something false. Nugget marks AI-generated content, shows each card's source, and lets you review everything before it is saved: use those checks, and compare important facts with your source material.
